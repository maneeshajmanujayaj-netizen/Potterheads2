Sacred Twenty-Eight

The House of Nott

Listed in the 1930s Pure-Blood Directory, the Nott family is one of Britain's oldest wizarding lineages. Cantankerus Nott is widely believed to have authored the Sacred Twenty-Eight list itself, while later generations—including Theodore Nott—became known for quiet intellect, solitary study, and complicated ties to the First and Second Wizarding Wars.

Sacred Twenty-Eight

The Ollivander Lineage

Crafting fine wands since 382 B.C., the Ollivanders are also counted among the Sacred Twenty-Eight. Garrick Ollivander revolutionized British wandcraft by standardizing three supreme cores—Phoenix feather, Dragon heartstring, and Unicorn hair—and teaching generations of witches and wizards that the wand chooses the wizard.

About Us

Potterheads began on May 24, 2024 (24.05.2024) as a wizarding home for Harry Potter fans in Sri Lanka. Just one day later, on May 25, 2024 (25.05.2024), our community welcomed its first 100 Wizards. That early spark grew to 1K Wizards by July 17, 2024 (17.07.2024), bounced back stronger when we rebuilt to 1K on January 13, 2026 (13.01.2026), and today brings together more than 1.5K Wizards.

Across our WhatsApp Channel and YouTube (@potterheadslk), we share House Cup competitions, daily Harry Potter trivia and "Would you rather" prompts, lore breakdowns, and magical lessons. All original content created for Potterheads is protected—please do not copy, alter, or forward our original posts or materials to other public channels without proper credit or consent.
